Rules & connection tips

Each player is one virtual computer. Complete 3 arrow sequences or one exact typing passage in 25 seconds. Wrong arrows restart the current sequence and cost 2 seconds. A wrong typing submission costs 2 seconds. Attacks and cleanups each award 10 points. Every infected, online enemy computer earns the attacking team 1 point per second. Cleanup gives that computer a 6-second shield. A 2-second action cooldown follows a success. No automatic infection spread occurs in competitive mode.

Keep your page open. Players are marked offline after 15 seconds without a check-in and stop earning hold points. Refreshing restores your session on this browser. One host laptop runs the server; stopping it clears all matches. Leave view only closes your interface; your computer goes offline after 15 seconds. Players stay on the roster for a rematch. Both teams should have equal numbers for a fair match.
